Input Behavior

League-style Attack Move: press Attack Move (X by default), then left-click to confirm. Attack Move Click (A by default) executes immediately at the cursor. Use the left-click toggle above to choose whether every left-click performs Attack Move Click.
Right-click — move to the cursor (green movement ping).
Move cursor to screen edges — pan the camera around the larger continuous arena (click the arena first to capture the cursor; press Esc to release it).
Attack Move (X) — press X, then left-click to confirm the command.
Attack Move Click (A) — immediately performs Attack Move at your cursor, like League’s Attack Move Click.
S — stop movement immediately.
R — reset the challenge and start a fresh round (customizable in Keybinds).
P — pause/resume the session.
Hold Space — keep the camera centered on your champion; release to unlock the camera (League-style).
Ranger’s Focus — land 4 hits to build Focus, then press its keybind (Q by default) to activate a 6-second flurry of five arrows per attack.
F — Flash toward your cursor.
Your movement speed — adjust how quickly your champion moves when right-clicking or chasing a target.
Fullscreen button — toggle fullscreen.
Left-click toggle — when enabled, left-click immediately performs Attack Move Click; when disabled, left-click only confirms Attack Move after pressing X. Right-click always cancels chasing and moves normally. Press A for Attack Move Click at any time.
Use Attack Move range to set when your champion can attack. If a selected target is too far away, your champion follows it until in range, then attempts the attack. Stop or right-click to cancel the chase. Toggle the range indicator on or off. Use Training bots to set 1–10 dummies and Bot durability to choose 1–200 hits per dummy. Bullet hell difficulty adjusts wave size, firing frequency, and projectile speed from Easy to Extreme. Target abilities / skillshots fires one aimed projectile per volley; triple-shot spreads are disabled. Mixed combines those shots with bullet-hell edge waves. Adjust Skillshot travel speed from 1 to 2200 to practice slower or faster projectiles: around 1200 for Morgana/Lux Q or 2000 for Ezreal Q. The same slider also changes bullet-hell projectile speed; bullet hell still sends dense waves from the screen edges. Use Edge camera pan speed and Camera zoom sliders to tune camera movement and view distance. The arena is now one continuous map instead of repeating 3×3 tiles. Hold Space to keep the camera centered on your champion; release it to return to edge-scrolling.

Attack Move selects the living target nearest your cursor within a balanced 425-unit radius. If no target is within that radius, it moves toward the cursor using the red Attack Move indicator. Aim accuracy is based on cursor-to-target distance: directly on or close to a target scores about 95–100%, and falls gradually farther away. A selected target takes one hit per attack; accuracy measures cursor precision, not whether the attack connects.
